目标函数（BPR-Opt）
\[\mathcal{L}_{\text{BPR}} = \sum_{(u,i,j)\in\mathcal{D}} \ln\,\sigma\!\left(\hat{x}_{ui} - \hat{x}_{uj}\right) - \lambda\|\Theta\|^2\]
预测得分 & 梯度
\[\hat{x}_{ui} = \mathbf{U}_u \cdot \mathbf{V}_i \qquad \sigma(x)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-x}} \qquad g = \sigma\!\left(\hat{x}_{ui}-\hat{x}_{uj}\right)\]
参数更新
\[\mathbf{U}_u \mathrel{+}= \eta\!\left(g(\mathbf{V}_i-\mathbf{V}_j)-\lambda\mathbf{U}_u\right)\quad \mathbf{V}_i \mathrel{+}= \eta\!\left(g\mathbf{U}_u-\lambda\mathbf{V}_i\right)\quad \mathbf{V}_j \mathrel{+}= \eta\!\left(-g\mathbf{U}_u-\lambda\mathbf{V}_j\right)\]

① 三方加权融合
\[\text{final}(u,m) = 0.93\cdot\text{user\_score} + 0.025\cdot\text{fair}(m) + 0.03\cdot\text{eta\_score}(m) + 0.015\cdot\text{supply}(m)\]
ETA 归一化
\[\text{eta\_score}(m) = 1 - \min\!\left(\frac{\widehat{\text{ETA}}(u,m)}{70},\;1\right)\]
② xQuAD 贪心重排目标（逐步选入 S）
\[m^* = \arg\max_{m\notin S}\;\Bigl[\alpha\cdot\widetilde{r}(m) + \beta\cdot\mathbf{1}[\text{cat}_m\notin \mathcal{C}_S] + \gamma\cdot\frac{1}{1+\ln(1+\text{cnt}_m)}\Bigr]\]
参数取值
\[\alpha = 1-\beta-\gamma = 0.84\qquad \beta = 0.12\;(\text{类目多样})\qquad \gamma = 0.04\;(\text{长尾促进})\]
归一化相关度
\[\widetilde{r}(m)=\frac{\text{score}(m)-\min_c\text{score}(c)}{\max_c\text{score}(c)-\min_c\text{score}(c)}\qquad\text{候选集} = \text{Top-80 by final\_score}\]

每次选出 m* 后将其类目加入已覆盖集合 C_S，下轮选择中同类目的 category_gain 归零，强制多样。
